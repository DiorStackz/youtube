# STYLE GUIDE

## Tone

- Calm
- Controlled
- Slightly mysterious
- Never excited or loud

## Voice

- Documentary narrator style
- No slang, no filler

## Sentence Style

- Short sentences
- Clean structure
- Easy to read aloud

## Rules

- No “what’s up guys”
- No long intros
- No overexplaining
- No hype words

## Goal

Make the viewer feel curious and slightly unsettled
